This is the Facebook pass for the Rent Searcher project. It is NOT one of the two
automated daily checks -- read WHEN THIS CAN RUN before anything else, because
this pass cannot work the way they do.

Everything here was verified by hand on 4 Sep 2026 against a live logged-in
Facebook session. Where a claim is a measurement, the measurement is given.


WHEN THIS CAN RUN
=================
Facebook is reachable ONLY through the owner's own logged-in Chrome, driven by
the `claude-in-chrome` MCP tools. Verified the same day:

- `curl` with a browser User-Agent -> HTTP 400 on www.facebook.com,
  m.facebook.com, mbasic.facebook.com and graph.facebook.com alike. Not 403,
  not a login redirect -- 400 for all four. There is no unauthenticated path.
- WebFetch -> login wall (unchanged since the note in the other two prompts).
- The Graph API is not an option either: Marketplace has no public API, and the
  Groups API that once allowed reading group posts was withdrawn by Meta.

`run_daily_check.ps1` invokes `claude -p --permission-mode dontAsk` -- a headless
print-mode session. It has no Chrome extension attached, and even if it did, this
pass drives the OWNER'S REAL BROWSER WINDOW: it would seize their Chrome at 05:00.
So do NOT wire this into the scheduler. Run it only in an interactive session,
while the desktop app is open, and preferably when the owner is not mid-work in
Chrome. Say in the final report that the pass ran attended.

Account risk, stated plainly because it is the owner's personal account: Facebook
detects and acts on automated browsing. The concrete loss is that their own
account -- the same one their Messenger conversations live in -- gets checkpointed
and locked behind an ID-verification wall. To keep that unlikely: read only,
never message a seller, never click "Notify Me" or any button that writes,
keep to a human pace (a few seconds between pages), and do one city per pass
rather than sweeping all eleven in a row.


WHAT WORKS
==========
URL parameters on the category feed do take effect (verified: `radius=25` changed
the page's own filter chip to "Within 25 km"):

  https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/{city}/propertyrentals/?sortBy=creation_time_descend&daysSinceListed=1&radius=25

City slugs seen working: `hanoi`. Sub-categories exist as their own paths and are
narrower than the parent -- `/marketplace/{city}/apartments-for-rent/`,
`/condos-for-rent/`, `/houses-for-rent/`, `/townhouses-for-rent/`.

The sidebar carries a **"Listings from individuals only"** switch. Turn it on --
but see the first pass's lesson below: it thins the agency reposts, it does not
remove them. Set it through the URL (`isC2CListingOnly=1`) rather than clicking.

Read the feed with `read_page` (accessibility tree), NOT with screenshots. Every
listing link carries a usable aria-label of the shape:

  "<title>, ₫<price>, <place>, listing <id>"   href="/marketplace/item/<id>/..."

so titles, ids and places parse straight out of it. Dedupe on the numeric id in
the href, exactly the way the Chợ Tốt passes dedupe on `/(\d{8,9})\.htm`.


LESSONS FROM THE FIRST REAL PASS (HCMC, 4 Sep 2026 -- 14 listings in, 2 usable out)
=================================================================================
- The "Listings from individuals only" switch writes `isC2CListingOnly=1` into the
  URL. Put it in the URL directly instead of hunting for the switch and clicking it.
  The full working feed URL is:
    /marketplace/{city}/propertyrentals?daysSinceListed=7&isC2CListingOnly=1&sortBy=creation_time_descend&exact=false&radius=30
  City slug `hochiminhcity` works, as does `hanoi`.
- **That filter does not actually exclude agencies.** With it on, the best find of
  the pass was posted by "Thảo (Khải Lộc Land)" -- an agency, with its brand in the
  ad text. Judge the seller from the body and the hashtags, not from the filter.
- **Read item pages with `get_page_text`, never by slicing `document.body.innerText`
  yourself.** Marketplace renders its own nav and a "Today's picks" rail into the
  body text, and hand-rolled slicing repeatedly returned the rail instead of the
  listing -- including prices belonging to completely different ads. `get_page_text`
  does the article extraction correctly.
- **Two different item-page formats exist.** The older one shows "· In stock",
  "Listed in <district>" and a `Details` block. The newer "Rentals" one shows
  "₫X / Month", "Rental Location" and a `Description` block -- and carries NO age at
  all, not even a relative one. So:
    * a listing taken from a `daysSinceListed=N` feed has a real upper bound on its
      age -- use N and say in the notice that it is a bound, not a publication date;
    * a listing taken from the "Today's picks" rail has NO age information
      whatsoever. Do not put it in the dataset. Report it to the owner directly
      instead. Writing a made-up number into `daysAgo` would be a lie in the one
      field this whole site is built on.
- The price trap is worse than "sellers are sloppy": it is systematic and usually a
  factor of 1000 or 10. Seen in one pass: ₫13,500 for 13.5 million, ₫1,400,000 for
  14 million, ₫4,000,000 for 40 million, ₫8 and ₫105 and "Free" for real rentals.
  Every price must be read out of the body text.
- Roughly one in seven listings survives. Of 14: three were sales sitting in the
  rentals category (5.5 tỷ, 5.45 tỷ, "5xty"), one was house-painting services,
  several had no price anywhere in the body, one offered several different units
  with no size for any of them. Two were usable. Budget for that ratio.
- The sidebar rail is worth reading even though its listings are undated: it
  surfaced an Ascentia 3-bedroom in Phú Mỹ Hưng, which is one of the owner's own
  three search areas, when the filtered feed had not. Harvest it for the owner, not
  for the dataset.


THE ITEM PAGE IS WHERE THE REAL DATA IS
=======================================
Open `https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/item/<id>/` and read it with
`get_page_text`. A real example from this run:

  title   Cho thuê chung cư mini tại 47 Nam Dư 30-35m2 full đồ
  price   ₫4,900,000
  place   Listed in Quận Đống Đa, Hà Nội        <-- WRONG, see below
  body    🏡 Địa chỉ : 47 Nam Dư, Hoàng Mai, Hà Nội
          Phòng 30m2-35m2, 4tr9 và 5tr2, máy giặt chung, cửa sổ to,
          full nội thất..., hợp đồng 12 tháng, cọc 1 đóng 1,5
  seller  Đỗ Ngọc Lan Oanh · Joined Facebook in 2014

That body is often BETTER than a Chợ Tốt ad: a street address, the contract
length, the deposit terms and the per-unit price spread all in one place.


THE FIVE TRAPS (all four seen in a single 24-listing sweep)
===========================================================
1. **The district field lies.** The example above says "Listed in Quận Đống Đa"
   while its own address is in Hoàng Mai -- a different district on the other
   side of the city. Facebook also prints "Location is approximate" on the map.
   Assign the project's district key from the ADDRESS IN THE BODY, never from
   Facebook's own place field, and never from the map pin.

2. **The price field is not a price.** Sellers type millions as plain numbers,
   so the feed shows "₫115" for an 11.5-million house, "₫11" and "₫12" for
   apartments, "₫6,688" for 6.688 million. Take the price from the title or the
   body text and convert it yourself. A price that came out of Facebook's price
   field alone must never reach a listing row.

3. **Sales are mixed into the rentals category.** "Park 2ngủ 75m nhỉnh 7tỷ" --
   7 tỷ is 7 billion đồng, a purchase price, sitting in Property Rentals. Any
   figure in tỷ is a sale. Drop it.

4. **Unrelated services are in there too.** "Sơn nhà giá rẻ" (cheap house
   painting) was the 8th result. The category is not self-cleaning.

5. **Dates are coarse.** There is no timestamp. The page carries relative ages in
   `abbr`/`time` elements -- "11h", "1w", "2w" -- and the feed labels the newest
   ones "Just listed". So "1w" means somewhere in 7-13 days. Use
   `daysSinceListed=1` (or 7) in the URL to bound it at the source, record the
   age honestly, and put the imprecision in the listing's `notice`: the project's
   14-day rule cannot be applied to a Facebook post with the precision it gets
   applied to a Chợ Tốt one.


PHOTOS: THE HARD CONSTRAINT
===========================
Facebook photo URLs are signed and short-lived. Measured on a live Marketplace
image on 4 Sep 2026: the URL's `oe` parameter decoded to 2026-09-08T16:25Z --
**four days**. Storing one in `details.photos` the way Chợ Tốt URLs are stored
would put a broken image on the site within the week.

Re-hosting them is not available either: the harness blocks signed/query-string
URLs from being returned out of the browser, so a photo URL cannot be handed to a
downloader. (This is a deliberate guard, not a bug to work around.)

So, until someone solves this properly, **Facebook listings go in without
photos.** Consequences to state in the run's report, not to paper over:
  - they render on the site without a gallery;
  - `post_new_to_telegram.py --min-photos 3` will never pick them up, so they
    are site-only and never reach the hub;
  - that is why the only Facebook listing on the site before this pass (one, in
    Đà Lạt) has no photos either.
If photos are ever wanted, the one workable route is `computer` screenshots with
`save_to_disk`, committed into the repo and referenced by relative path -- heavy,
manual, and it only works on GitHub Pages, not inside the Artifact. Do not start
that without asking the owner first.


THE PASS
========
1. Ask before starting if the owner is using Chrome; this takes over a tab.
2. `list_connected_browsers` -> `tabs_context_mcp{createIfEmpty:true}`.
3. Open the category feed for ONE city with `sortBy=creation_time_descend`,
   `daysSinceListed=1`, a radius that covers the city, and switch on
   "Listings from individuals only".
4. `read_page` the feed. Collect id + title + place from the aria-labels.
5. Drop anything already on the site: load the built page with
   `site_data.load_listings()` and match on the Facebook item id in the URL.
6. Open each surviving item page, `get_page_text`, and read it as a human would.
   Apply the five traps above. Discard anything whose body gives no address --
   that rule is already the site's and it disqualifies a large share of
   Facebook posts.
7. Write the rows the same way every other batch does: `new_listingsNN.py`,
   ids from `allocate_ids.py`, `source="fbmarketplace"`, bilingual `desc`
   and `descEn`, a `notice`/`noticeEn` naming the date imprecision and the
   absent photos, url `https://www.facebook.com/marketplace/item/<id>/`.
   No `details.photos`.
8. There are no source coordinates. Facebook's pin is deliberately approximate,
   so do NOT put anything in `chotot_coords.json`; let the normal geocoder work
   from the address text in the description, same as any Batdongsan row.
9. `python rebuild_final.py` -> pin pipeline -> rebuild -> commit -> push, exactly
   as the other prompts describe.
10. Close the tab you opened (`tabs_close_mcp`) before finishing.


GROUPS
======
Facebook groups (source key `facebook`) are richer than Marketplace for long-term
rentals but were not exercised in this pass. They are reachable the same way once
Chrome is driving, at `https://www.facebook.com/groups/<id>`, and the owner is
already a member of the relevant expat/rental groups. Same traps apply, plus one
more: group posts are conversations, so the price and address are often in a
comment rather than the post. Treat a post whose own text lacks an address as
disqualified rather than digging through comments.
